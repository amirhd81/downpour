# Downpour privacy policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Downpour is built to keep what you download private.

## What stays on your Mac
Your downloads, download history, links, site logins, settings and everything else you do in Downpour stay on your Mac. Site logins are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Downpour has **no tracking, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting**.

## What Downpour connects to
- **The sites you download from**, to download your files.
- **GitHub**, to check for Downpour updates and, if you download videos, to download and update yt-dlp, Deno and FFmpeg. Only standard information that comes with any web request (such as your IP address) is shared.
- **The browser extension** talks only to the Downpour app on your own Mac (127.0.0.1). It doesn't send your browsing anywhere else.

## Your email (only if you give it)
If you join the early-adopter list, the email address you type is sent to a Google Form owned by the developer and saved in a Google Sheet. Google's own privacy policy applies to how Google stores it.

It's used **only** to tell you when Downpour Pro launches and to give you the early-adopter discount. It's never sold or shared, and you won't get unrelated mail.

## Removing your email
Open an issue on this page titled **"Remove my early-adopter email"**. Don't write your email in the issue: we'll reply there with a private way to confirm it, then delete it.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
