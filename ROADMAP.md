# Downpour roadmap

Downpour is in **public beta** and actively being built. This page shows what works today and what's coming next. Plans can change based on your feedback.

Have an idea? [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/amirhd81/downpour/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Something broken? [Report a problem](https://github.com/amirhd81/downpour/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), or use **Help › Report a Problem…** in the app.

**Legend:** ✅ available now · 🔨 being built · 📋 planned · 💡 being considered

---

## ✅ Available now (1.0 beta)

### Downloading
- ✅ Multi-part downloads (up to 32 parts) that re-split the biggest remaining part, so every connection stays busy
- ✅ Pause and resume, even after restarting your Mac
- ✅ Automatic retry when a connection drops
- ✅ Refresh an expired download link without starting over
- ✅ Add many links at once, including number ranges like `photo[001-100].jpg`
- ✅ Watches the clipboard for download links
- ✅ Site logins for password-protected downloads, and proxy support
- ✅ Import and export your download list

### Browser extension
- ✅ Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and Vivaldi
- ✅ Catches browser downloads, with a window to start, pause or queue them
- ✅ "Download with Downpour" in the right-click menu
- ✅ Grab every link on a page and pick the ones you want
- ✅ Sites to leave alone (their downloads stay in the browser)
- ✅ Updates itself when Downpour updates

### Video
- ✅ Video downloads from thousands of sites, with quality and playlist options
- ✅ A download button on videos, only on supported sites
- ✅ Streaming video (HLS), including encrypted streams
- ✅ Tools installed and kept up to date for you, only if you choose to download videos

### Scheduling and speed
- ✅ Scheduler queues: drag downloads in, set the order, start and stop times, and how many run at once
- ✅ Sleep your Mac or quit Downpour when a queue finishes
- ✅ Speed limits, limits only during certain hours, priorities and fair sharing between downloads

### Organising and safety
- ✅ Categories that sort files into folders by type, fully editable
- ✅ Filter the list by file type, and search
- ✅ Checksum checking (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512)
- ✅ Downloaded apps get the same macOS security check as in Safari
- ✅ Keeps your Mac awake while downloading
- ✅ Download speed on the Dock icon, and a menu bar icon
- ✅ Notifications when downloads and queues finish
- ✅ Automatic updates

## 🔨 Next up

- 🔨 **Extract archives automatically** (ZIP, RAR, 7z, multi-part), with an option to delete the archive afterwards
- 🔨 **More video options:** audio only, choose the format, download subtitles
- 🔨 **Scan downloads for viruses** with a scanner you choose
- 🔨 **Signed and notarized app**, so macOS no longer asks you to confirm the first time

## 📋 Planned

- 📋 **BitTorrent and magnet links**
- 📋 **Downpour Pro** official launch, with a free 7-day trial (beta early adopters get 80% off)
- 📋 **Browser extension in the Chrome Web Store**, for one-click installing
- 📋 **Remote control from your phone:** add and manage downloads from any device on your home network
- 📋 **Shortcuts and AppleScript support** for automating downloads
- 📋 **Translations**, starting with Persian

## 💡 Being considered

- 💡 Whole-site grabber with filters, and scheduled re-sync
- 💡 Download one file from several mirrors at once
- 💡 Preview videos while they download, and peek inside ZIP files before downloading
- 💡 Tags and Spotlight search for downloads
- 💡 RSS and podcast feeds that download new episodes automatically

---

## Not planned
Some features other download managers offer won't come to Downpour, because they break websites' rules or mostly help with pirated content:
- Solving captchas or getting around file-host limits
- Built-in torrent search
